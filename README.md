# Hotel Booking Cancellation Insights

This repository provides an analysis of hotel booking cancellations based on different dimensions. The visualizations help identify patterns and trends in customer behavior, room preferences, and booking statuses. Below are the insights derived from the dashboard.

---

## Key Metrics and Visualizations

### 1. **Overall Booking Status**
- **62.96%** of bookings were **not canceled**, while **37.04%** of bookings were **canceled**.
- The data highlights a significant percentage of cancellations, potentially indicating areas for improvement in customer satisfaction or booking processes.

### 2. **Hotel Types**
- **City Hotels** accounted for **66.45%** of the bookings.
- **Resort Hotels** represented the remaining **33.55%**.
- City Hotels seem to be the preferred choice for most customers.

### 3. **Market-wise Booking Status**
- Cancellations are most prominent in the **Online TA** and **Offline TA/TO** segments.
- **Corporate** and **Direct bookings** show lower cancellation rates.
- Tailored strategies can be designed for specific market segments to reduce cancellations.

### 4. **Guest Type Booking Status**
- **Couples** dominate the booking segment, followed by **single** and **family** bookings.
- Cancellation rates vary by guest type, with **single travelers** showing a slightly higher cancellation tendency compared to families.

### 5. **Room Preference and Allotment**
- Customers with **room allotment mismatches** (not getting their preferred room) had a higher cancellation rate compared to those who received their preferred room.
- This suggests improving room allocation processes can reduce cancellations.

### 6. **Monthly Booking Trends**
- The peak booking months are **August**, **July**, and **October**, while **January** and **December** experience the lowest booking counts.
- The seasonal trend is evident, with higher activity during the summer and early fall.

---

## Features of the Dashboard
- **Interactive Filters**: Toggle between hotel types (City Hotel or Resort Hotel).
- **Comparative Analysis**: View differences between booking statuses and cancellations across multiple dimensions.
- **Time-based Trends**: Analyze monthly booking behaviors.

---

## Tools and Technologies Used
- **Visualization**: Created and Processed in Power BI for dynamic insights.
- **Dataset**: Includes 119K hotel booking records from city and resort hotels.

---

## Recommendations
- **Enhance customer retention strategies** for the Online TA market segment by offering incentives or flexible policies.
- **Streamline room allocation systems** to minimize dissatisfaction leading to cancellations.
- Focus on promoting Resort Hotels during off-peak months to balance booking distribution.
